# ❤️ Cardiovascular Disease Risk Dashboard (Tableau)

👉 **Where can I see the tool?**
You can explore it live here:
[Cardiovascular Disease Risk Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/shared/PTCCRX7N4?:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

---

## 1. **How do I use it?**

The dashboard is interactive and allows you to filter the data.

**Available Filter:**

* **Age Group**

<img width="1021" height="431" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/03f3c576-1259-46bd-b95a-37fdc68a96dd" />


When you select an age group, the dashboard updates to show:

* **Heart Disease** → distribution of Yes/No cases in that age group
* **Sex** → male vs. female distribution
* **Smoking History** → smoking status linked to the group
* **BMI** → average body mass index
* **Consumption patterns** → alcohol, fried potatoes, fruits, and green vegetables

All visualizations update automatically when the filter changes.

---

## 2. **Why does it matter?**

Cardiovascular disease risk is influenced by **age, diet, BMI, smoking, and lifestyle behaviors**. Understanding how these variables vary by age group helps in:

* Identifying at-risk populations
* Designing targeted prevention programs
* Promoting healthier choices based on evidence

---

## 3. **What is the key question?**

* **Which age groups show varying levels of consumption and characteristics associated with cardiovascular disease risk?**

---

## 4. **What data is behind it?**

The dashboard is built on health data containing:

* Heart Disease (Yes/No)
* Sex
* Smoking History
* BMI
* Alcohol Consumption
* Fried Potato Consumption
* Fruit Consumption
* Green Vegetables Consumption
* Age Group

📌 Kaggle Data Source: [Cardiovascular Diseases Risk Prediction Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/alphiree/cardiovascular-diseases-risk-prediction-dataset)

---

## 5. **What does it show?**

By filtering for each age group, users can explore:

* Which age groups have higher or lower **heart disease prevalence**
* How **BMI and smoking history** vary with age
* Which groups have greater **alcohol and fried potato consumption**
* Which groups have healthier **fruit and vegetable consumption**

---

## 6. **Who is it for?**

This dashboard is designed for:

* **Public health researchers** — to analyze consumption and disease patterns
* **Healthcare professionals** — to target interventions by age group
* **Policy makers** — to design prevention programs based on age-linked risk factors
* **Educators** — to raise awareness about lifestyle risks for heart disease

---

## 7. **What’s next?**

This Tableau dashboard serves as a **visual decision-support tool** to:

* Identify age groups with higher cardiovascular risk
* Compare health behaviors across demographics
* Inform preventive strategies for healthier populations
